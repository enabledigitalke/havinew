Know before you play

Rules of the table.

Show up ready, play fairly, and respect the competition and every player.

Strict rules

  • Players must be at the pool-table area by their designated time.
  • 5 minutes late: the player loses one game.
  • 10 minutes late: the player is disqualified.
  • Matches follow the announced format: best of 3, race to 2.

Final rules & sportsmanship

  • Prize money is secured in a designated account and is not to be touched.
  • The loser-pays rule applies on the day of games: the loser covers table fees.
  • Follow the tournament organiser's instructions and respect match decisions.
  • Come ready to compete with fair play and good sportsmanship.
